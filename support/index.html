Help & Contact

Support

For help with Wird, email ossacodes@gmail.com.

What To Include

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • The app version and build from Settings > About.
  • A short description of what happened.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if you know them.

Common Actions

  • Edit reminders: Settings > Reminders.
  • Enable notifications: Settings > Notification Settings.
  • Clear local data: Settings > Data.
  • Clear downloaded Quran content: Settings > Data > Clear Quran Downloads.
  • Review privacy details: Settings > Privacy & Data.

Response Time

Support requests are handled by email. Response time can vary based on volume and issue complexity.
