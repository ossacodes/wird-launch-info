Local Data

Data & Deletion

Wird does not create user accounts. Most app data is stored locally on your device.

Delete Local App Data

Open Wird and go to Settings > Data to clear progress, history, Quran reader settings, bookmarks, downloads, reminders, or all local app data.

Delete The App

You can also delete Wird from your device through iOS. Deleting the app removes local app data stored in the app container, subject to normal iOS backup and restore behavior.

Support Email Data

If you emailed support and want a support message removed from support records, email ossacodes@gmail.com from the same email address and include your request.
