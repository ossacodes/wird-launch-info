Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Wird stores, what network services it uses, and how to contact support.

Information Stored On Your Device

Wird does not require an account. The app stores dhikr progress, personal dhikr sets, completed session history, reminder times, Quran bookmarks, reader preferences, theme settings, and downloaded Quran text or audio on your device.

You can remove local app data from the Settings screen.

Network Services

The Quran reader can connect to Quran.com and Quran Foundation services to refresh Quran text, translations, transliterations, word timing metadata, and recitation audio. These requests are made so the app can display and play Quran content. Those services may receive standard network information such as your IP address, device request metadata, and requested content URLs.

Wird does not use these requests for advertising or tracking.

Notifications

If you add a daily reminder, Wird asks iOS for notification permission. Reminder times are stored on your device and scheduled through Apple's local notification system. You can delete reminders in the app or disable notifications in iOS Settings.

Analytics, Advertising, And Tracking

Wird does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs in the current release.

Support Emails

If you email support, you choose what information to include. Support emails may include your email address, message contents, and any diagnostic details you decide to send.

Children

Wird does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date will be updated when a new policy is published.
